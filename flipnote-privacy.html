Privacy Policy — FlipNote

Last updated: October 11, 2026

FlipNote (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is an Android application developed by JRAltd Inc. (package name: com.flipnote). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and outlines how user information is handled.

1. Offline-First Architecture & Data Storage

FlipNote is built with an offline-first, local-storage architecture designed to protect your privacy:

  • Notes and Content: All notebook entries, titles, notes, drawings, paper styles, and passwords are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's encrypted Room (SQLite) database and Encrypted DataStore.
  • No Server Uploads: We do not operate proprietary servers to store, mirror, sell, or analyze your notes. Your content stays on your personal device.
  • Media and Attachments: When you add images or drawings to a note, file references remain local on your device. No photos or files are transmitted to external servers.

2. Authentication and Credentials (Optional)

FlipNote offers an optional digital identity verification feature powered by Google Identity Credential Manager:

  • Credential requests and token verifications are processed on-device and directly through Google Play Services.
  • We do not collect or maintain external user account records or credentials on external servers.

3. Internet and Permissions

FlipNote requests the Android Internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET) solely for:

  • Communicating with standard Google Play Services (such as in-app reviews and Credential Manager flows).
  • Opening external user-requested web pages (such as opening this privacy policy or the Google Play Store listing in your device's browser).

FlipNote does not include any third-party marketing trackers, telemetry SDKs, or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain full control over it at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete any individual note, vault note, or doodle directly within FlipNote.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing app data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > FlipNote > Storage > Clear Data) or uninstalling the app permanently erases all databases, passwords, and preferences from your device.

5. Security

FlipNote leverages Android's application sandbox security, isolating its storage from other apps. In addition, sensitive notes can be secured in the password-protected Vault screen with hashed credential verification.

6. Children’s Privacy

FlipNote does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for general audience use.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated “Last updated” timestamp.
