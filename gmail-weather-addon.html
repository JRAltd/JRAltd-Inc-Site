Gmail Weather Addon

A Google Workspace Gmail Add-on that shows live weather, a short forecast, and a plain-language weather summary right inside Gmail — without leaving your inbox.

What it does

Gmail Weather Addon adds a weather panel to Gmail's sidebar. Search any city or zip code to see current conditions, and save a location as a favorite so the add-on opens straight to it next time. Switch between °F and °C at any time.

What it doesn't do

The add-on does not read, scan, or store the subject or body of any email. It only uses the location you search or save. See the full privacy policy for details.

Related

There's also a Chrome extension version of this tool, Gmail Weather Companion, with the same weather features plus an interactive Doppler radar map.
