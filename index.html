Outlook Add-in

Email Sticky Notes

Microsoft Outlook Mail Add-in

A 100% free, self-contained Microsoft Outlook add-in to attach private, color-coded, timestamped sticky notes, interactive action checklists, priority badges, and reminders directly to any email or conversation thread. Pin the taskpane open to easily review and manage notes as you triage your inbox — all stored privately in your mailbox with zero tracking.

  • 6 color themes & 4 priority badges
  • Interactive action checklists
  • Pinnable taskpane (stays open across emails)
  • Global cross-email search & tags
  • 100% private (Exchange mailbox storage)
  • Dark mode & JSON/Markdown backup

How to Install in 30 Seconds:

  1. Download the Manifest: Save the email-sticky-notes-manifest.xml configuration file using the download button below.
  2. Open Outlook Add-in Installer: Go to aka.ms/olksideload in your browser (or in Outlook Web / New Outlook for Windows, click Apps → Add apps → Manage your apps).
  3. Upload the File: Under Custom add-ins, click Add a custom add-in → Add from file... and select the downloaded email-sticky-notes-manifest.xml file.
  4. Pin & Use: Open any email, click Email Notes in the ribbon or action bar, and click the Pin icon in the top right so your notes remain visible as you switch emails!
